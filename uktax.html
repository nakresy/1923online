Disclaimer: This calculator gives an estimate for illustration only and is not tax, financial or legal advice. It uses the standard Personal Allowance and the Income Tax rates for England, Wales and Northern Ireland, and employee (Class 1, category A) National Insurance. Savings interest uses the Personal Savings Allowance only (£1,000 for basic rate, £500 for higher rate), and any interest above it is taxed at 20% or 40% depending on which band your salary falls in; the starting rate for savings and interest that spans two bands are not included. It does not include Scottish rates, pension contributions, student loan repayments, benefits in kind, other income or any tax code adjustments. Your actual pay may differ. Please check the current rates on GOV.UK or speak to a qualified adviser.
